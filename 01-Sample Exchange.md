Follow the procedure based on the specific holder you are using. First you will load your sample into the TEM holder, and then load the holder into the TEM. 

# Loading specimen into TEM holder


## Loading Samples into Single Tilt TEM Holder
![Single Tilt Holder Image](images\single_tilt.png)
1. The "cartridge" where the sample is secured is removable by lifting the spring loaded "alligator clip" using the tool showed in the figure above. The cartridge can then be lifted and moved onto the white plastic chuck. The small metal pin on the plastic chuck should go through the small hole on the back end of the cartridge (away from the sample) to prevent the cartridge from sliding around. 
2. Sligtly loosen the 2 screws, swivel the copper clamp with tweezers to reveal the 3mm specimen cup. Remove any sample in the cup using vacuum tweezers. 
3. Place your sample in the cup, and swivel the copper clamp back on top of the sample. 
4. Tighten the screws using a flat head mini screwdriver to secure the sample in place up to finger tight. Do not over tighten. 
5. Load the cartridge back into the holder by lifting the spring loaded "alligator clip" while simultaneously inserting the cartridge into the mouth of the clip. You can use a gloved hand to lift and position the cartridge. There is a small pin inside the alligator clip, which should go through the hole in the cartridge.
6. Check under an optical microscope to see if the sample is secure, and the cartridge is completely flat. 
7. Check for fibers on the o-rings under the optical microscope. 

## Loading Samples into Double Tilt EDS Holder
WARNING: This holder is made using Beryllium metal for X ray transparency. Beryllium can corrode when wet, and is extremely toxic.
![Double Tilt Holder Image](images\double_tilt_holder.png)
![Double Tilt Holder Detailed Image](images\double_tilt_detailed.png)
1. Place the plastic sample exchange mount on the specimen holder stand. 
2. Place the sample holder on the exchange mount with the guide pin facing downwards. The bottom surface of the sample retaining region should be supported by the plastic sample exchange mount. 
3. Partially loosen 2 screws which hold down the copper retaining arms (called leaf springs) with a small flathead screwdriver. Swing the leaf springs away from the sample cup by gently poking them with a tweezer. 
4. Remove the spacer and the specimen plate. These parts are very precious and easily misplaced, so keep them safely on the holder stand, preferrably on the white plastic support chuck for the holder tip. 
5. If there is a sample in the holder, remove that. If not, place your sample in the empty 3mm sample retaining cup. 
6. If using a thin TEM grid (such as a copper mesh grid), place the spacer onto the sample. This is not needed if you are using a sample thicker than 100 micron such as a SiN grid. 
7. Place the "specimen fixing plate" onto the sample such that the wings of the specimen plate face towards the retaining arms. This plate helps distribute pressure evenly accross your sample and can only work in the orientation shown in the detailed holder view above. The wings of the spacer should sit in the grooves on the holder. If the specimen fixing plate orientation is rotated 180 degrees, the wings will still fit in the grooves, but tightening the screws will bend the sample cup because the wings are located off-center.  
8. Place the retaining leaf springs on top of the wings of the specimen plate by gently poking and lifting with a sharp tweezer.
9. Tighten the two screws to finger tightness. 
10. Remove the sample rod from the loading cradle, and flip the rod over to make sure your sample does not fall out. You can gently tap the rod with a tweezer about 1 inch away from the sample region.

## Loading Samples into 3 sample high tilt holder
This holder needs special care-- samples can be loaded using the special tools which come with the holder. The holder can be moved between the 3 sample positions by turning the knob at the back of the holder. 

1. Move the sample to position 1.
2. Place the holder into the cradle with the guide pin facing down. 
3. Insert the plastic sample support chuck under the holder to make sure sample position 1 is supported. 
4. Place your sample in the sample cup. 
5. Place the spacer into the sample cup. This is not needed for Silicon nitride grids > 100um thickness. 
6. Place the specimen fixing screw into the cup. 
7. Use the 3 pronged screw driver to turn the specimen fixing screw into the sample cup. ![Three Sample Holder](images\ThreeSampleHolder.png)
8. Move the holder to position 2 by turning the knob at the back of the holder, and repeat the process for your second sample. Then move the holder to position 3, and load your third sample. 
9. Retract the holder to position 1 before loading into the TEM.  

# Loading a TEM holder into the TEM

 Once a sample has been loaded onto a TEM holder, the holder can be loaded into the microscope using either manual mode, or using the automated specporter. The specporter only works for specific holders (where the Specporter OK icon is observable in TEM Center software).

## Loading holders manually
### Precautions 
Before any sample exchange make sure the following are true:
1. Stage is in neutral position. 
2. Beam valves are closed. 
3. EDS detectors are retracted.
4. Objective (HC) aperture is retracted. 
5. Dectris Arina is retracted AND the high voltage on the sensor is turned off. The lighning bolt icon in the TVIPS software should show the word "Off" under the lightning bolt. 

![Goiniometer](images\Goiniometer.png)

1. Ensure the third (bottom) button on the goiniometer panel says "Manual".  This means the holder will be loaded manually. If it says auto, push it till it says "Manual".
2. Align the holder mini pin with the groove at the 9-o clock position, and insert the holder till you reach a physical stop. **DO NOT TURN THE HOLDER YET**.
3. Push the button which says "Air" on the goiniometer panel. This will change the button to "Pump", and the Diffusion Pump will start pumping on the TEM holder. The pump button will be flashing. The pressure to observe is PiG4 in the "Valve Vacuum Monitor" page. Because V8 and V21 should be open (green), PiG4 will drop over time.
4. If using the EDS double tilt holder, plug in the cable for the beta tilt into the goiniometer plug lablled "TL-Y".  
5. Once PiG4 reaches 36uA, the holder can be inserted into the column. To do this, you need to follow this sequence:
  a) do a short clockwise turn till you reach a physical stop,
  b) short insertion till you reach a physical stop,
  c) long clockwise turn till you reach a physical stop,
  d) long insertion till holder is in the column.
6. In the "Open Project" window, select the correct TEM holder. If you do not select a TEM holder, the nanospace map (stage navigator) will not work correctlly.
7. If using the double tilt holder, plug the cable into the TLT-Y socket on the goiniometer.

![Schematic for inserting TEM rod into column](images\tem_insertion.png)

## Loading holders using the specporter

Loading and unload using the Specporter only works for holders which are appropriately calibrated. These holders will have a "Specporter OK" mark on the holder image in TEM Center software, when you open a new project window. Do not touch the holder when the automatic insertion or retraction is ongoing. 

### Precautions 
Before any sample exchange make sure the following are true:
1. Stage is in neutral position. 
2. Beam valves are closed. 
3. EDS detectors are retracted.
4. Objective (HC) aperture is retracted. 
5. Dectris Arina is retracted AND the high voltage on the sensor is turned off. The lighning bolt icon in the TVIPS software should have the word "Off" under the lightning bolt. 


The specporter is designed to load the TEM holder into the column by moving the goiniometer instead of moving the holder, which is particularly useful when using cryo holders. To load the holder using the specporter, do the following:
1. Open goiniometer door fully. Make sure this stays fully open during sample insertion. 
2. Make sure the "Auto" button is active on the goiniometer panel. The groove in the goiniometer should be at the 12 o clock position if auto insertion is enabled. If not, press the button which says "Manual" till it changes to "Auto". You can also do this in a couple of places in the TEM Center software. 
3. Align the guide pin with the groove into the goiniometer till you reach a physical stop.  This should start the pre-pumping. When using a double tilt holder, connect the TLT-Y cable at this point. 
4. Select appropriate holder in the Open Project window in TEM center. The "Specporter OK" logo must be present for the holder to be loaded using the specporter. ![Open Project](images\OpenProject.png)
5. This should load the holder automatically.

# Retracting a TEM holder
![Schematic for TEM rod retraction](images\tem_retraction.png)

## Retracting a TEM holder manually
### Precautions 
Before any sample exchange make sure the following are true:
1. Stage is in neutral position. 
2. Beam valves are closed. 
3. EDS detectors are retracted.
4. Objective (HC) aperture is retracted. 
5. Dectris Arina is retracted AND the high voltage on the sensor is turned off. The lighning bolt icon in the TVIPS software should have the word "Off" under the lightning bolt.
6. If using the 3 sample holder, move back to sample position 1.  

7. Ensure the third (bottom) button on the goiniometer says says "Manual". If it says auto, push it till it says "Manual".
8. Use the following sequence of steps :
  a) Pull the holder out till you reach a physical stop. 
  b) Turn the holder counter clockwise (about 75 degrees) till you reach a physical stop. 
  c) Pull the holder out a very short distance, till you reach another physical stop. 
  d) Turn the holder counter clockwise (about 15 degrees) till you reach a physical stop. 
  e) Press the button which says "Pump" so that it says "Air". This will vent the load loack. 
9. With a gloved hand, hold the TEM metal rod at the point closest to the goiniometer, while pulling the rod out with your other hand holding the back of the TEM holder. 
10. Remove your sample from the holder, and load the holder in the correct cradle safely. 
## Retracting a TEM holder using specporter

### Precautions 
Before any sample exchange make sure the following are true:
1. Stage is in neutral position. 
2. Beam valves are closed. 
3. EDS detectors are retracted.
4. Objective (HC) aperture is retracted. 
5. Dectris Arina is retracted AND the high voltage on the sensor is turned off. The lighning bolt icon in the TVIPS software should have the word "Off" under the lightning bolt. 
6. If using the 3 sample holder, move back to sample position 1.  


The specporter is designed to load and unload the TEM holder into the column by moving the goiniometer instead of moving the holder, which is particularly useful when using cryo holders. To remove the holder using the specporter, do the following:
1. Fully open the goiniometer cover. 
2. Enable automatic removal by pushing the Auto/Manual button on the goiniometer till the button says "Auto".
3. Click "Auto" in the SpecPorter settings window in TEM Center. 
4. In the "open project" window of TEM Center, to the left of the holder icon, check the Operation is set to Auto (not manual). 
5. Press the Insert/Remove button on the goiniometer panel. ![Insert/Remove Button](images\InsertRemove.png)
6. When the sequence finishes, the carrier plate will return to its original position, the pump/air switch will say "Air", "Insert Ready" will be displayed on the Insert/Remove switch, and the icon indicating the holder will disappear from the TEM Center Detectors/Aperture tab. 
7. With a gloved hand, hold the TEM metal rod at the point closest to the goiniometer, while pulling the rod out with your other hand holding the back of the TEM holder.
8. Remove your sample from the holder, and load the holder in the correct cradle safely.
