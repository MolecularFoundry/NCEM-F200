# Voltage Centering in TEM mode

This step can be through of as a coarse adjustment of the beam tilt. 

1. Set magnificaiton to 100kX. 
2. Press std focus, and focus sample using [z height](Z height.md) buttons on stage rotation hand panel. 
3. Select the "Bright Tilt" switch on the left hand panel. 
4. Converge the beam with brighness and center it via shift X/Y. 
5. Spread the beam larger than the 1/2 of fluorescent screen. 
6. Turn on HT wobble (right hand panel). Select bright tilt on the left hand panel, and using the DEF/STIG knobs on left and right, adjust the beam tilt till the image expands and contracts concentrically. 
7. Turn off HT wobble, converge the beam, and shift to center with Shift X/Y knobs. 
8. Repeat steps 5-7 till aligmnents converge.
