## Ramping HT and FEG Emission

### Turning on HT
If you come to the microscope and find the HT is currently not at the value you would like, do the following:
1. Click on the HT button in TEM Center Detectors/Aperture window. 
2. If your desired voltage is 80kV, turn on the EL short by clicking 100. If your desired voltage is 200kV, make sure the EL short is off. The EL short options might not be visible, so make sure to expand that menu.
3. Select the desired target HT (only 80kV and 200kV are available)
4. Make sure the HT ON button is green. If not, click it. 

### Changing Voltage
1. If you're changing voltages, you always need to turn the HT off first.
2. Change the EL short. If you're going to 80kV, you need the EL short on (select 100).
3. Turn the HT back on. 

### Turning on Emission
1. Click on the "Emission" button in the TEM Center Detectors/Aperture window.
2. Perform a high flash by pressing `High` next to flashing. A pop up window will ask if you are sure, click yes. 
3. The button labelled "Auto Emission" may be in the "Off" state, wherein the "Off" button will be green. Click on the "On" button to turn on emission. 
4. A popup window will ask if you want to start Auto Emission. Click "Yes".
5. Bringing up emission will take about a minute or two. The Emission "On" button will be flashing green till emission is fully turned on. 
6. Once emission is turned on, an emission current will be read in the TEM Center Detectors/Aperture window. This is should about 14-15 uA right after emission is brought up. 

## Adding Liquid Nitrogen
If the column vacuum (SiP3) is below 4E-5 Pa and your sample does not outgas much, there is no need to use liquid nitrogen, but if the vacuum is high:
1. Wear PPE.
2. Retrieve a dewar full of LN2 from the station outside NCEM. 
3. Remove the Anti Contamination Device (ACD) heater from the liquid nitrogen tank on the left side of the column. 
4. Cover the viewing screen. 
5. Add LN2 to the LN2 tank. Wait 10-15 minutes for the pressure to drop.
