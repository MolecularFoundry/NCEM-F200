# Basic EDS procedures 


## Holder safety
When in doubt, check with NCEM staff about compatibaility of your holder with EDS detectors. The JEOL single and double tilt holders are compatable with EDS. Any in-situ holders need to be double checked. For the 3 sample holder, use EDS detector #1 only. 

## Alignment notes 
1. For EDS, we usually operate at a high beam current (low spot number).

## Inserting EDS detectors
1. Find EDS detectors: The SSD detectors can be a little tricky to find. Minimize TEM center. If you don't see the floating OP panels, on the desktop there are two icons for software `SSD1` and `SSD2.` Click on these. If the software is already open, you'll get an error `This program is running.`  If they are minimized, you can find the icons in the bottom right menu bar.
2. Insert EDS detectors by clicking first `retract` if they are in the `s-change` position and then `insert`.

## Collecting EDS maps in digital micrograph
1. Make sure you are in STEM SI mode
2. Insert hard x-ray aperature (labeled HX) on JEOL PC if you want. This can be helpful for minimizing spurious signals and background counts.
3. Start by collecting a dark field image in the scan tab
4. In the SI acquisition pane, click on `EDS` and then `2D Array`. This will let you draw a field of view to scan. You can specify step size and box size. 
5. In general, it's sensible to do a low collection time and multiple passes but it will depend on your sample. 
6. If you're doing multiple passes, you may want to do drift correction. Make sure the `Drift` is selected as well as the `BF-DF`, so a simultaneous image is collected for drift correction.

## Shut down (before removing your sample)
1. Remove hard x-ray aperture if inserted
2. Move EDS detectors to sample exchange position. First click `retract`. When these buttons change from yellow to green, then click on `s-change`
