
# Centering C2 aperture
The goal of this alignment is to make sure that your C2 aperture is centered about the TEM optical axis. There are multiple ways to do this which should reach the same aperture position. 

## Centering Method 1
1. Insert the desired C2 Aperture and pick a magnification around 30-50kX.
2. Converge beam to cross over with brightness knob on left hand panel.
3. Center the spot on the screen with shift X/Y knobs. 
4. Spread the beam with brightness clockwise till about 1/4 of the flu screen is illuminated. 
5. Center the beam by moving the aperture position using the touch screen panel. Make sure CL aperture is selected. 
6. Repeat 2-5 till the beam doesnt swing side to side as you wobble brightness knob through crossover. 

## Centering Method 2:
1. Insert the desired C2 and pick a magnification around 30-50kX.
2. Continuously wobble the brightness knob through crossover using your left hand while adjusting the aperture position using the aperture position buttons on the touchscreen display above the left hand panel. 
3. First use the up/down buttons: move the aperture up till it is obviously misaligned and swinging back and forth as you wobble brightness. Then move the aperture down till it is again obviously misaligned and swinging back and forth as you wobble brightness. Then find the approximate mid point of these 2 positions. 
4. Repeat step 3 but for left and right aperture position buttons. 
5. When you finish, as you move to eithr side of crossover, the beam will only expand and contract, but not move side to side.
