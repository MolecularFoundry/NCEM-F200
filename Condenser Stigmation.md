# Condenser Stigmation 
In TEM and STEM, the purpose of this aligment is to create a rotationally symmetric illuminated area (or probe) on your specimen. 

## In TEM mode:
Choose the spot size you want, and correct the condenser stigmation by clicking the COND/STIG button on the left hand panel and using the DEF/STIG knobs on the left and right hand panels. The beam should not be elliptical when spread on either side of crossover when condenser stigmation is good. It is often easiest to start this on the fluorescent screen, and the tweak this on the clearview camera which you are using the image. ** Do not converge the beam on the clearview camera, as that will damage the sensor.**
