  VME is the communication between TEM Center and all boards. Resetting this is pretty safe and should solve many communication issues. This does not bring down HT. The VME is its own computer, which can be accessed via remote login from the JEOL PC on IP 172.16.41.1 . Nobody knows what VME stands for! 

- Press the Reset MAIN button on the right side of the TEM console (near your right knee).
- Wait till MAIN button is illuminated
- Wait 5 minutes
- Press Reset MAIN button again, and the LED should be off. If LED is illuminated, TEM center can not be started. 
- Wait 5 minutes
- Restart TEM Center
