# HT Conditioning

## Precautions
- This is a **STAFF ONLY OPERATION**.              
- HT must be off before switching the HT Conditioning Handle, and the column valves are off. 

## Procedure
1. Turn off HT.
2. Climb to the back of the scope, near the gun. 
3. The conditioning handle has 2 positions: operate (at 6 o clock), and COND (at 4 o clock). If you are in the operate position, turn the white handle such that the red dot is at the 7 o clock position, and then psuh the white handle in. 
4. Once it is pushed in all the way, rotate the white handle counter clockwise, till it the red dot is at the 3 o clock position, and then retract the white handle all the way. 
5. Once retracted, you should be able to rotate the handle to the  4 o clock position (ie, the conditioning state). 
6. This now allows you to press the HT Cond button in TEM Center, which takes 2 hours. 
7. Once HT Conditioning is done, a message pops up saying do not touch the handle for 1 minute and HT will ramp back down to 0kV. 
8. It is a good idea to open and close the beam valve with the HT off a couple of times after conditioning is completed. 
9. Once HT has ramped back down, move the position of the conditioning handle back to the operate position.
10. Then ramp the HT back up to 200kV.
