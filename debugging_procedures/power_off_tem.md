# Powering off TEM
1. Worry about gatan, dectris. We did not turn off Dectris, but we did power off the Clearview Camera via GMS software and Digiscan box in the chase room.
2. Turn down HT.
3. Turn Power switch (near right knee of operator) to OFF state. 
4. Wait > 10 minutes. There is a 10 minute timer for diffusion pump to cool down. 
5. In the rack in the  chase,  in the middle of the rack, there are on/off switches (white buttons). Press the on button to restart.
6. Power up the TEM PC.
7. Restart TEM Center
8. You will need to reset Lenses (switch near right knee), by turning off and back on.
