# Imaging in Low Magnification TEM
1. Make sure the SiP3 in the Valve/Vacuum Monitor screen is below 4E-5Pa. 
2. Make sure the operation and illuminationa are in TEM mode. 
3. Push the Low Mag button on the right hand panel. 
4. Turn the Beam ON using the button on the left hand panel, which opens the column valve. 
5. Adjust the illumination witht the brighness and shift knobs. 
6. Adjust magnification with the Mag/CamL knob on the right hand panel. 
7. Select the desired magnification, and image using the flu screen camera or with the Gatan Clear View camera. 

## In case there is no beam
1. Remove the objective (HC) and SA aperture. 
2. Lower magnification. 
3. Move specimen with track ball.
