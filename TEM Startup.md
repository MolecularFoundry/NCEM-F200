## Ramping HT and FEG Emission

### Turning on HT
If you come to the microscope and find the HT is currently not at the value you would like, do the following:
1. Click on the HT button in TEM Center Detectors/Aperture window. 
2. Select the desired target HT (only 80kV and 200kV are available)
3. Make sure the HT ON button is green. If not, click it. 

### Turning on Emission
1. Click on the "Emission" button in the TEM Center Detectors/Aperture window.
2. The button labelled "Auto Emission" may be in the "Off" state, wherein the "Off" button will be green. Click on the "On" button to turn on emission. 
3. A popup window will ask if you want to start Auto Emission. Click "Yes".
4. Bringing up emission will take about a minute or two. The Emission "On" button will be flashing green till emission is fully turned on. 
5. Once emission is turned on, an emission current will be read in the TEM Center Detectors/Aperture window.
