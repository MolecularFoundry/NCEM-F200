# Tilt Balance
These are the pivot points which ensure that when you tilt the beam, you do not introduce beam shifts. 
1. Click the Lens/Deflector button under the Detectors/Aperture panel. 
2. Scroll over the `wobbler` button and click the `Wobbler Window` button. 
3. Change magnification to about 100kX, center the beam, and bring it to crossover..
4. Click the `tilt X` wobbler, and use the DEF/STIG X button to make sure you dont see split spots on the screen. Uncheck tilt X wobbler.
5. Click the tilt Y wobbler, and use the DEF/STIG Y button to make sure you dont see split spots on the screen. Uncheck tilt Y wobbler.
