# Tilt Balance
These are the pivot points which ensure that when you tilt the beam, you do not introduce beam shifts. 
1. Click the Lens/Deflector button under the Detectors/Aperture panel. 
2. Scroll over the wobbler button and click the "Wobbler Window" button. 
3. Bring a feature into the field of view at a mag of about 40-60kX.
4. Click the tilt X wobbler, and use the DEF/STIG X button to make sure the particle remains steady. Uncheck tilt X wobbler.
5. Click the tilt Y wobbler, and use the DEF/STIG Y button to make sure the particle remains steady. Uncheck tilt Y wobbler.
