## Z Height in TEM Mode

Press std focus, and focus sample using z height buttons on stage rotation hand panel. You can do this by one of 2 methods:

    a) Spread the beam and bring an object into the view (either using the binoculars or flu screen camera). 
    b) Start the image wobbler (with button on right hand panel). You will see a dual image of the object. 
    c) Move the sample up or down till the two images converge into one image.
    d) Stop the image wobbler once you are done. 

Alternatively, you can also do the following:

    a) Bring the beam to crossover with the brightness knob. 
    b) Move the stage in X-Y till your beam is parked over a sample. You see a halo of scattering (which can at times look like a diffraction pattern if your sample is crystalline) on the fluorescent screen. Make sure your sample is thin enough for electrons to penetrate through the thickness. 
    c) Move the sample in Z till the entire halo of scattered electrons converges into the sharp spot at crossover. You may need to reposition the stage if the beam moves off the sample as you are moving in Z. 
    d) Spread the beam, and you should be reasonably close to good focus.
