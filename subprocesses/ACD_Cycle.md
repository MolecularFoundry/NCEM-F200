After using the microscope, if you use LN2, it is advisable to do an ACD cycle if you are the last user of the day. ACD Cycles can be done once a week, and the LN2 tank will last 12 hours. If you are sure someone will come in the next morning, refill the tank if you are the last user of the day. If you are unsure, follow the procedure below to do an ACD cycle

1. Remove TEM holder from column. Make sure emission is off and beam valves are closed.
2. Retract all detectors (including EDS detectors). 
3. Put the cap on the viewing screen of the scope. 
4. Wear cryo PPE. 
5. Remove the anti condensation cap on the LN2 tank.
6. Insert the heater, and align the two metal plugs with the black connector sockets. Push down till the cylinder stops.
7. In TEM Center, select the Maintenance panel, and click the ACD Heat button. 
8. Click start, this will need 8 hours.
