# Gun Alignment : once a week
1. Set mag to 40kX. 
2. Select bright tilt. 
3. Set spot size to 5.
4. Converge the beam and center it. 
5. Shift the beam to the middle with the shift knobs.
6. Go to spot 1. 
7. CLick on GUN in the aligment panel (software).
8. Converge the beam and use the shift knobs to center the beam. 
9. Repeat steps 2-8 till the beam does not move as you go from spot 1 to 5.

These gun aligments for each angle value are indepndent of each other, so you can redo the process at angle 1-5.    

# Tilt/ Shift Balance (Pivot point): done rarely
The purpose of this alignment is to decouple beam tilt and beam shift, ie, when you tilt the beam, you want to avoid introducing a shift in the illuminated area (and vice versa).
1. Set Mag to 40kV and converge the beam to crossover with brightness knob.
2. Click the "Compensator Tilt" -- not sure if this is CL Compt Tilt or IS Comp Tilt. 
3. In the wobbler window, click the "Wobbler Tilt X"
4. Minimize wobble with DEF/STIG X knob. 
5. Center the beam with the Shift knobs. 
6. Repeat steps 3-5 till wobble is reduced. 
7. Turn off Tilt X wobbler, and turn on tilt Y wobbler. Converge beam to crossover.
8. Minimize wobble with DEF/STIG Y knob.
9. Center beam with shift knobs.
10. Repeat steps 8-9 till wobble is reduced. 

The next part if the shift purity alignment, which is done even less frequently (Only if  doing centered DF TEM ). 
1. Select SA DIFF on right hand panel and set Camera Length to 200cm. 
2. Select PLA and center the spot via DEF/STIG knobs.
3. Click the Wobbler ShiftX button in the Wobbler window. 
4. Click the "Compensator Shift" -- not sure if this is CL Compt  or IS Comp .
5. Minimize wobbling with the DEF/STIG X knob.
6. Turn on Wobbler ShiftX, and turn on Wobbler Shift Y. 
7. Minimize wobble with DEF/STIG Y. 
8. Select BRIGHT TILT and Center the spot with the DEF/STIG knobs. Do not use PLA here!

# Intermediate stigmation alignment
1. Select SA DIFF and set Camera Length to 200 cm.
2. Turn the brightness all the way to clockwise and obtain a caustic spot with diff focus button. 
3. Select PLA and center spot with DEF/STIG
4. Click the IL STIG in Knob Assignment, and correct the astimatism to obtain a 3 fold symmetric caustic spot.
